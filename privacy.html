PRIVACY

Website privacy information

This portfolio uses a contact form and Google Analytics 4 to support professional inquiries and understand general website usage.

Contact form

When a visitor submits the contact form, the information they provide is sent to eduardo.caride0121@gmail.com through the configured form-delivery service. The information is used only to review and respond to the inquiry.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is installed to measure page visits and interactions such as resume views, downloads, project case-study views, contact submissions, and booking-link clicks. Analytics information is intended for the private use of the site owner.

External services

The booking link may open an external scheduling service. The contact form uses FormSubmit for email delivery. Those services may apply their own privacy practices.

Contact

Questions about this website can be sent to eduardo.caride0121@gmail.com.
